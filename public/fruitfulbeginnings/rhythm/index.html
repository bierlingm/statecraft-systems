Our daily rhythm

A steady, unhurried day.

Small children settle when they know what comes next. Morning, midday, and afternoon each have their part.

  1. Morning

    Arriving and settling. Your child comes in, and the morning finds its pace.

  2. Midday

    Time outside in the yard, and a meal at the table.

  3. Afternoon

    Rest, stories, and play. Then you come for them.
