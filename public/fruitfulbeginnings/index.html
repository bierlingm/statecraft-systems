Tell Laura The place

From Laura

The childhood I had, offered here.

I grew up in a large Christian family in rural Idaho. There was always a full table, room to run, and older and younger children growing up together.

A country childhood is not a compromise. It is one of the best starts a child can get. I opened my own home so your child can have that start: a small group, on an organic blueberry orchard below Rattlesnake Mountain.

Good beginnings, like anything growing, need good ground, sun, patience, and care.

Laura

Help paying

Licensed and DCYF approved.

Fruitful Beginnings is licensed and approved by the Washington State Department of Children, Youth, and Families. Families who qualify can get help paying, and your child receives the same care as everyone else.
