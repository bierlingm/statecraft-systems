The day care

Care that feels like a home.

Laura Sabin runs Fruitful Beginnings in the Prosser and Benton County area. She grew up in a large Christian family in rural Idaho.

The day care is a small group of children aged 3 to 5 who are potty trained. It is a cozy home — home-cooked meals, a large yard, small class sizes — rather than a sterile center.

Fresh air, room to roam, and nature close by.

The home is in a rural setting on an organic blueberry orchard, up against Rattlesnake Mountain. That place is part of the care: fresh air, room to roam, and nature right there.

Families from town are welcome. Many families nearby work at local farms, within about 5 to 8 miles.
