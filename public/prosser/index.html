How it runs.

Nothing here needs setting up. This is what happens from now on, and the one or two taps that are yours.

Automatic

A guest asks

You and Ben both get an email and a text with the guest’s name, dates and party size (never their message) and one button: Open this request. That page checks Airbnb, Vrbo and the website calendar for a clash and works out the all-in price: $194 a night, $150 cleaning, 10.7% Washington lodging tax.

One tap · you

You accept

One tap holds the dates for 2 days, takes them off the website calendar and the Airbnb/Vrbo feed, emails the guest the price and your Venmo link, and texts you both what went out. Whoever taps first handles it; the other sees who did.

Instant Book stays on, and Airbnb reads our calendar only every few hours. So for a direct booking, also block those dates on Airbnb by hand right after you accept. It takes a minute and closes the gap.

One tap · you

They pay, you confirm

The guest presses I’ve sent the payment and you get a text with one button. Check Venmo, tap it, and the booking is final. If no money comes, the guest is reminded a day before the hold ends, then the dates go back on sale by themselves and you’re told.

Automatic

The stay looks after itself

The guest hears from you four times: a week before; 9am on arrival day with the address, door code, Wi-Fi, parking (three cars in the driveway), bins and check-in from 4pm; checkout morning (out by 10am); and a thank-you the day after.

If nobody answers a request, you’re both texted again after 12 hours, and after a day it comes to Moritz too.

Good to know

  • Owner page. theprosserhome.com/manage shows every request, hold and direct booking. Sign in with your email (we send a button, no password) and that phone stays signed in.
  • First booking email. If it lands in spam or promotions, mark it “not spam” and add bookings@theprosserhome.com to your contacts.
  • Changing something. A new door code, Wi-Fi password, price or hold length: text Moritz. The door code and Wi-Fi go straight into the system and never onto a page like this one.
  • Cards alongside Venmo. You said “ask me again after a few bookings”, so we will.
