Is the house described accurately?

Confirm each bed size, which rooms are upstairs, any stair or access considerations, pet conditions, quiet hours and the current amenities.

Tyler + co-owner

Are these the photos we should use?

Confirm permission to reuse the listing photos. New daylight photos of both suites, the upstairs living area, kitchen and patio could help guests understand the layout.

Tyler

Which local places would you recommend?

The guide has a researched starting set. Tell us your favorites and any places to remove.

Tyler + co-owner

How should guests book at launch?

The current design sends guests to Airbnb. Decide whether the first launch also needs direct inquiries, or a full booking and payment flow.

Before launch

Who receives requests and keeps the site current?

Confirm the guest contact details, request recipient, response expectations and who manages updates. If enabled, the calendar needs the private Airbnb export URL.

Direction already in place.

Current working choices from the design review; owner sign-off is still ahead.

  • Use the actual house and its layout as the basis of the website.
  • Lead with real house photography and the cream-and-green design.
  • Remove small uppercase section labels and repeated sales copy.
  • Give named local stops useful Maps links.
  • Keep the upstairs kitchenette, fireplace and game room visibly distinct in the amenities list.
  • Keep version history and feedback alongside the project.
