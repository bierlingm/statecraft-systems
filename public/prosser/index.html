Payments

Live now: the Pay page opens Venmo to @TJ-SAB with the amount and dates filled in. Money lands in that Venmo; you move it to the business account. Venmo’s rule: a personal profile may only take payment for a stay if the guest marks it as a purchase (2.99% to you, and the guest gets purchase protection). A free Venmo business profile is cheaper (1.9% + $0.10) and every payment counts as a purchase automatically.
