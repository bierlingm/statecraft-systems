Your questions, answered.

From your message on 13 September.

Built

1. Can it sync with the Airbnb and Vrbo calendars?

Yes. Both platforms publish a private calendar link for each listing, and the website now reads both and greys those nights out. It refreshes every 30 minutes.

The other direction is covered too: a booking taken on the website goes into a private calendar feed that you import once into Airbnb and Vrbo, so those calendars block the dates as well. Airbnb refreshes imported calendars roughly every 3 hours, Vrbo similarly. For a guest arriving within a day or two, block the dates by hand on both platforms as well; the owner page reminds you.

If direct bookings grow and you want that lag gone, a channel manager such as OwnerRez (about $40 a month) connects through the official Airbnb and Vrbo interfaces and can take over this layer later without changing the website.

What we need from you: the calendar export links from Airbnb (Calendar → Availability settings → Connect calendars → Export) and Vrbo (Calendar → Import & export → Export).

Built

2. What does a payment link cost?

Nothing to add it. PayPal keeps its usual fee on each payment, currently 3.49% plus $0.49 for goods-and-services payments in the US, and nothing else changes on our side.

How it works for a guest: they pick dates and send a request, you reply within a day with the all-in price (cleaning fee and the 10.7% Prosser lodging tax included; on direct bookings you remit that tax yourselves), and the reply links to the new Pay page with the amount filled in. They pay through PayPal, you record the booking on the owner page, and the dates are blocked everywhere.

Business or personal account? We recommend a free PayPal Business account: it lets the payment show the house name instead of a personal name, supports invoices and receipts, and keeps rental income separate for tax time. A personal account works with the same link, but guests would see your name, and PayPal's rules require rental payments to go through goods and services either way, so the fee is the same.

What we need from you: which account, and its PayPal.Me handle (paypal.me/yourname).
