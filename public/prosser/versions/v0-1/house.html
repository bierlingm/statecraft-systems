The house

A real home, five minutes from everything.

Built as a family house and kept that way. Three bedrooms, two full bathrooms, an open kitchen and living room, and a back yard made for long evenings.

Kitchen & dining

Cook like you live here.

A full-size range and oven, big fridge, dishwasher, and a proper set of knives, pans and bakeware. Drip coffee and a French press, a kettle, a toaster, and a wine fridge that fits a weekend's worth of tasting-room finds.

The dining table seats six, and there's bar seating at the island for breakfast. Prosser's Saturday farmers market is a short walk away from May through October.

Outside

Warm nights were made for this yard.

A covered patio with a dining table and a gas grill, a fire pit with seating for six, and a fenced lawn. Prosser gets around 300 sunny days a year and summer evenings stay warm long after dark.

Off-street parking for two cars, with room for a boat or a trailer if you're heading to the river.
