Explore Prosser

The birthplace of Washington wine.

Prosser is a small farm town on the Yakima River with an outsized wine scene: dozens of tasting rooms, three AVAs within half an hour, and a calendar of festivals that fills the town every few weeks from spring to fall.

Wine

Thirty tasting rooms, no designated driver required.

Start at Vintner's Village, a walkable cluster of a dozen tasting rooms five minutes from the house. Then head east to the Red Mountain AVA (about 20 minutes) for some of the state's most celebrated Cabernet, or south into the Horse Heaven Hills.

  • Vintner's Village Airfield Estates, Milbrandt, Thurston Wolfe, Alexandria Nicole Cellars and more, side by side.
    5 min drive
  • Walter Clore Wine & Culinary Center Rotating tastings from across Washington, right on the river.
    6 min
  • Mercer Wine Estates & Hogue Cellars Two of the valley's founding names, both with Prosser tasting rooms.
    5–10 min
  • Red Mountain AVA Col Solare, Hedges, Kiona, Fidelitas and twenty others on one small hillside.
    20 min

Eat & drink

Small town, good plates.

Downtown is a five-minute walk. Coffee and pastries in the morning, a brewery or a tavern in the evening, and a couple of dependable spots for a proper dinner after a day of tasting.

  • Brewminatti Coffee, breakfast and live music in a historic downtown building.
    Walk
  • Horse Heaven Hills Brewery Local beer and pub food.
    Walk
  • Bern's Tavern Burgers, steaks and a classic small-town bar.
    Walk
  • Chukar Cherries The original store for the Northwest's favorite dried cherries and chocolates.
    5 min
  • Prosser Farmers Market Saturdays, May through October, downtown.
    Walk

Outdoors & events

Beyond the tasting room.

Yakima River

Float, paddle or fish the river that runs along the edge of town. Launches and a riverside walking path are minutes away.

Great Prosser Balloon Rally

Late September. Dawn launches over the river, a night glow, and the Harvest Festival downtown. Book early.

Festival season

Scottish Fest in June, the Wine & Food Fair in August, Thanksgiving in Wine Country in November. Something most months.

Getting here

Easy from anywhere in the Northwest.

Prosser sits right on I-82, roughly halfway between Yakima and the Tri-Cities.
