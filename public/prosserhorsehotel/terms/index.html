Provider: Statecraft Systems (statecraft.systems), a trade name of  ,  , represented by Moritz Bierling ("we").

Client:  ,  , Prosser, WA, represented by Ben   and Tyler   ("you").

Covered projects: Prosser Horse Hotel (prosserhorsehotel.com), The Prosser Home website, and any future project we both agree in writing (a text or email is fine) to add.

1. One-time setup: $250

We set up and verify your listings on Google Business Profile, Bing Places and Apple Business Connect, plus the horse directories HorseMotel.com, Horse Layover and Stable Weather, and we set up billing. Listings are created under an email address you control (for example listings@prosserhorsehotel.com), with us added as a manager. When setup is done, we hand you a simple record of every account: site, login email, renewal date and cost.

2. Monthly Care Plan: $50/month

  • Maintenance: keeping the covered sites online, secure and working; updates; fixing anything broken; checking forms and the intake room.
  • Small changes: updating text, rates, photos, hours, dates and similar on the existing sites and listings.
  • Listing upkeep: keeping your listings accurate when your details change, and handling renewals.
  • Special efforts (see section 3).
  • Response: we reply within 2 business days, and same day or next business day if a site is down.

3. Special efforts and the monthly cap

A special effort is a self-contained task that's bigger than a quick edit but smaller than a project. Examples: adding a new page or section, a seasonal promotion, a new directory or listing, a change to a form or the booking flow, or sorting out a problem with a third-party service on your behalf.

Care Bank of 3 hours per calendar quarter (about 1 hour a month), shared between small changes and special efforts and tracked in 15-minute steps. No more than one special effort per month, each up to 2 hours. Unused time expires at the end of each quarter and doesn't roll over. We'll tell you before you reach the cap. Extra time is $75/hour, and only with your OK in advance.

4. Out of scope (quoted separately)

New websites, redesigns, new features or functionality, larger content or photo projects, paid advertising, and anything expected to take more than about 2 hours. We send a short written quote (fixed price or estimate) first. Nothing is charged until you approve it.

5. Third-party costs (at cost, no markup)

Domain renewal (currently $10.46/yr), directory fees (about $60/yr), and any paid tools or services are charged to you at exactly what they cost.

  • Preferred: you pay the provider directly with your own card, in your own account.
  • Otherwise: we pay and add the cost as its own labeled line on your next monthly invoice, with the receipt.
  • Pre-approved: the recurring costs listed above.
  • Approval threshold: any new cost over $25, or more than $50 in third-party costs in a month, needs your OK by text or email before we buy it.

6. Billing and authorisation to charge your card

You save a card through a secure Stripe link. By saving it, you authorise Statecraft Systems to charge that card automatically for:

(a) $750 when you accept: the $500 website fee plus the $250 setup fee;

(b) $50 each month after that, starting one month after you accept, on the same day of the month;

(c) approved third-party costs (section 5) and approved extra time or quotes (sections 3–4), added to the next monthly invoice or charged separately when they come up. The amount is the receipt amount, or the amount you approved.

You get a receipt for every charge. You can update your card, view invoices or cancel through Stripe's customer portal. If a payment fails, Stripe will retry it and we'll let you know. If a balance stays unpaid for 14 days, we may pause work until it's settled. Prices don't change without 30 days' written notice.

7. Cancellation

The plan runs month to month. Either of us can cancel with 30 days' written notice (email or text is fine). The last month is billed as usual, any approved third-party costs and extra time already incurred are billed, and then charges stop. The setup fee is non-refundable once setup work has begun.

8. Ownership and handover

  • You own your domains, website content, photos, text, data and every account and listing set up for you, whether or not you stay on this plan.
  • Domain: prosserhorsehotel.com is currently registered through our Cloudflare account and held for you. On request, we will help you transfer it to a registrar account in your own name within 10 business days, apart from registry rules (for example, transfers aren't allowed within 60 days of a registration, transfer or contact change). Reasonable handover help is included in the plan.
  • We may use the sites as portfolio examples unless you ask us not to.

9. The fine print

We do our best, but we can't guarantee that third-party services (hosting, Google, directories, Stripe) will always be available. Each side's liability is limited to the fees paid in the previous 3 months. Either side keeps the other's private information confidential. This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Washington. Changes must be in writing (email or text counts).
