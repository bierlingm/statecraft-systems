Simple, self-contained stays

Dry camping.
Bring what you need.

There are no electrical, sewer or RV water hookups. Arrive prepared to use your rig’s own systems.

  • Approximately 3 acres for trailer parking and RV camping
  • Space for approximately 6 horse trailer / RV setups
  • Horse boarding reserved separately
  • Monthly stays by approval and arrangement

Parking or camping?

Parking: one horse trailer and tow vehicle are included with a boarding reservation, including when you sleep at a nearby hotel.

Camping: staying overnight inside your RV or LQ trailer adds the camping rate. Your rig uses one shared space, not a parking space plus a second camping space.

Additional trailers and oversized rigs require approval. Maximum rig dimensions and entrance directions are still to be confirmed.
