The essentials, covered

Built around horses.

  • 6 individual paddocks, each with shelter and water access
  • Heavy-duty steel pipe fencing, approximately 5 feet high
  • 160′ × 240′ sand-bottom riding arena
  • Small pasture for turnout
  • Approximately 7 acres of rideable property

Bring the rig, too

Parking comes with it.

One horse trailer and tow vehicle are included per boarding reservation. Multiple horses traveling in one rig do not need a separate parking allocation for every horse.

Stay at a nearby hotel and leave the truck and trailer here, or add RV/LQ dry camping if you sleep in your rig. Additional trailers and oversized rigs require approval.

Parking and camping share approximately six physical spaces. Included parking is still subject to space availability.
