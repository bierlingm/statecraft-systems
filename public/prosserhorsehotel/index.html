Recommendation

This is a focused local hospitality business with a credible website brief. Build the seven requested pages around real facilities, straightforward rates and one reservation journey. Trial Beds24 first for reservations and direct payments. Keep monthly stays approval-only. The critical dependency is shared trailer capacity: a polished website is straightforward; reliable combined booking must be proven before it is promised.

$500 can reasonably support a lean template-based site, supplied content/photos, basic SEO and standard booking setup if the trial fits without custom inventory work. It cannot honestly guarantee every combined-booking rule, custom owner editing, cross-platform integration and indefinite support. Agree the scope after a short feasibility gate. No paid accounts have been opened and no customer booking system is live.

What carries over from the earlier projects

The Prosser Home provides the useful model: a separate review room, visible status, small owner decisions, mobile-first pages, review history and practical handover. Its request/approval and delayed calendar model was appropriate for one rental, but its custom Venmo/booking code should not become this project's reservation engine: Ben explicitly wants existing software and card payments, with thirteen physical resources.

PNW Mobile Homes demonstrates the value of writing the interpretation and commercial boundaries down before building. Its expanding platform scope is the lesson to avoid: this room does not quietly turn a $500 hospitality website into a custom property-management platform. Settled facts are stated; questions ask only for missing policies or assets. The room has its own identity and leaves both earlier rooms intact.

The business and guest experience

Lead with a place for the horses, a place for the rig, and somewhere for the people to sleep. Rodeo travelers and haulers need fast answers on a phone: dates, number of horses, rig size, overnight arrangements, total price and arrival instructions. Sturdy fencing, sheltered paddocks, water, the large arena and the rural setting are persuasive concrete facts. Actual photography should demonstrate them; no invented testimonials or substitute ranch photography.

Separate included parking from sleeping in the rig. Free parking with boarding does not imply free RV/LQ camping. State dry camping beside the price and booking choice, including the absence of all three hookups. Describe the fifth wheel's own utilities separately. Publish an exact access map only once the address, entrance, rig clearance and turning area are confirmed. Do not infer distance to I-82 or Tri-Cities.

Inventory: the requirement that decides the software

Name paddocks H1–H6, physical rig spaces R1–R6 and the stationary fifth wheel F1. A three-horse group takes three paddocks but normally one rig space. Six different one-horse groups could use all paddocks and all rig spaces. Free parked rigs, paid camping rigs and rigs whose occupants sleep in the fifth wheel must draw from the same R pool when they use those spaces.

Camping is a paid use of a space, not a second space added to the free parking reservation. The same rig must never be counted twice. Conversely, parking cannot be a harmless untracked extra: six parked rigs leave no room for a seventh camping guest. Confirm whether the fifth wheel sits outside the six spaces and whether detached trailers/tow vehicles change the practical limit. “Approximately six” must become an exact capacity before instant booking.

Beds24 documents room dependencies, but those alone do not establish that an arbitrary number of horses can reserve exactly one shared parking unit per group. That conditional, many-to-one rule is unverified. First try explicit selection of one zero-priced parking unit or one paid camping unit alongside paddocks, with linked inventory for the two uses. Prove that the engine requires the rig allocation, preserves it across payment/cancellation and cannot oversell it. Do not rely on frontend instructions alone.

If this cannot be configured in the existing engine, use an owner-approved request workflow for combinations and explain the limitation before taking money. Single-resource instant booking is acceptable only where shared parking is guaranteed. A separate custom transaction layer is outside the proposed $500 scope.

For one night: two horses + camping = $95; two horses + fifth wheel + one dog = $200, before any approved taxes or additional charges. Parking is included once; horses do not count as household pets. These are illustrative arithmetic, not a live quote.

The monthly boarding price is 50% below four weekly blocks, and care terms change at monthly stays. Confirm this is intentional. Define a month (28/30 nights/calendar month), rollover dates, 8–13 nights, partial weeks/months, extensions and whether prices include tax. Do not silently select the cheapest combination of rates: six nightly horse nights cost $210 versus a $200 week, so the boundary matters. Two pets for a 30-night fifth-wheel stay would add $1,200 under the supplied rule; confirm that monthly stays use the same pet charge or an approved alternative.

Monthly boarding is explicitly self-care. Nightly and weekly feeding, watering checks, mucking out, turnout, bedding and emergencies are unspecified. Water access alone does not settle daily care. Decide these before guests reserve. Add check-in/out windows, late arrivals, cancellations, no-shows, deposits, refunds, damage terms and maximum rig size. Owners should confirm horse-health documentation, emergency contact, quarantine/separation and any waiver requirements with their adviser; this assessment does not invent a legal policy or local tax rate.

What fits the proposed $500

Proposed included scope, subject to your agreement: one restrained mobile template; seven concise pages; supplied photos processed for the web; clear rates/rules/contact/directions; one existing booking-engine configuration and embed if the trial passes; monthly request flow; page titles/descriptions, sitemap, search verification once access is available; owner walkthrough and a short handover guide. One consolidated review round is a scope proposal, not an agreed contract.

Separate cost or later scope: subscriptions, domain purchase/renewal, professional photography, paid ads, ongoing maintenance, custom booking code, complex inventory integrations and advanced automated messaging. Owner photo/content editing remains a launch requirement; it is not quietly deferred to “later.” A managed visual website editor is the leanest default, but the platform, exact plan cost and seven-page/custom-domain support must be quoted before purchase. If a static Cloudflare build is selected instead, a usable owner editor must be included and tested or the scope explicitly renegotiated.

If the trial fails, pause the instant-booking promise and present a specific revised scope/cost. Do not ship a request-only site and describe it as meeting the direct reservation/payment brief. No completion date is committed until photos, policies, domain access and payment verification are ready.

Pages, identity and search

Home leads with horse boarding and the three stay options. Horse Boarding explains paddocks, fence, arena, care and included parking. RV/LQ Camping emphasizes dry camping and rig limits. Fifth-Wheel Rental shows actual interior/exterior images, utilities, adult/child/pet limits. Rates & Reservations is the single date/horse/rig/lodging journey. Gallery uses real captioned photos. About & Contact provides contact details, hours, map and large-rig directions.

Use cream, oxblood, sand and dark brown with restrained serif headings, clear body type and simple fence/arena geometry. Western character should come from materials and photography, not cursive type, cowboy clip art or claims of safety certification. The project-room motif is illustrative, not a map or business logo.

Map search terms to useful pages instead of repeating them everywhere: Home for horse hotel Washington/Prosser horse motel; Boarding for overnight and monthly horse boarding; Camping for horse-friendly RV camping/overnight trailer parking; Contact for I-82 access and the Tri-Cities region where accurate. Unique titles/descriptions, canonical URLs, responsive compressed images with dimensions/alt text, sitemap and appropriate business structured data are foundations. Google Search Console verification needs owner access. Google Business Profile ownership, correct address/contact/category and real reviews are valuable separate owner tasks. No ranking or traffic guarantee.

Acceptance checks before launch
  • Book three horses with one camping rig; allocate three H units and one R unit, with correct total.
  • Book horses plus fifth wheel and pets; charge pets once; allocate parking where needed.
  • Fill R1–R6 using both free parked rigs and camping; reject the seventh. Never allow a paid/free alias to sell the same space twice.
  • Two simultaneous attempts for the final paddock/rig/fifth wheel cannot both confirm. Editing, cancelling and refunding release only the correct inventory.
  • Failed or abandoned payment never produces a paid confirmation; retry never duplicates the charge. Verify receipt, business payout path and cancellation/refund in isolation.
  • Reject 3 adults, 3 children, 5 people and 3 pets in the fifth wheel. Test weekly boundaries and monthly approval.
  • Import a channel block, add a direct stay, check the other calendar and document synchronization delay. No global no-double-booking claim based on iCal alone.
  • Owners independently change a rate, photo and paragraph; block dates, add a manual booking, find a reservation and manage cancellation/payment.
  • Check small-screen booking, keyboard navigation, readable prices, map/contact links, image loading, metadata, sitemap and Search Console verification.
