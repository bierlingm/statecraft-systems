Version 1.0: three directions

The same four screens in three looks: homepage, search (preset to “3+ bed, 2 bath, under $180,000, within 200 miles of 98901”), a home’s page, and the dealer sign-up. The data is sample data and the photos are drawn stand-ins.

A · Oversize Load

Black and road-sign yellow from the banner every one of these homes travels behind. Bold, industrial, unmistakably this market.

B · Evergreen

Warm paper, serif type, large photos, and your voice up front. A trusted regional guide rather than a listings site.

C · Market board

Dense, list-first and fact-forward, like Kayak. The same model from different dealers shows up side by side.

In all three:

  • Every card shows who is selling and when the listing was last checked.
  • Must-be-moved and factory-order homes are marked.
  • Homes from a dealer’s own website link back to it.
  • The dealer sign-up is the page you send to dealers.
