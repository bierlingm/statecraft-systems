The problem

Fluent is not the same as reliable.

Language models are remarkable at producing plausible text. Plausibility is cheap. What an organisation actually needs to know is narrower and harder: is this true, is it permitted here and now, who answers for it, and what happens if it is wrong?

The model vendor does not carry that risk. You do. Regulators, courts, auditors and the public will ask why a decision was made, and "the system suggested it" is not an answer.

  • Unstated assumptions slip through as facts
  • Evidence burdens go unexamined
  • Jurisdiction and authority are ignored
  • No record survives to explain the choice

How it works

Every candidate is a hypothesis until it survives testing.

Runcible reads a proposal for what it really asserts, surfaces the gaps, and checks it against five questions before anything consequential happens.

TruthDoes the evidence support it?
PossibilityCan it actually be done?
EthicsShould it be done?
AuthorityIs it lawful here, now, by whom?
LiabilityWho bears the consequence?

Five bounded verdicts. Never a shrug.

Warrant

It holds. Safe to rely on within the stated bounds.

Repair

Close, but overreaching. Here is the strongest version that does hold.

Block

It fails a test that cannot be fixed. Do not proceed.

Escalate

Beyond the authority of this decision point. Route to a person who holds it.

Undecidable

The evidence cannot settle it yet. Stated plainly, with what would.

Each verdict is written to a Decidability Record: what was claimed, what was tested, what was missing, and why the outcome followed.

Worked example

A product label, from draft to record.

Marketing proposes the line Clinically proven to reduce X. Here is what happens next.

  1. Clarify

    "Clinically proven" implies controlled trials, a defined population and a measured effect. "Reduce" needs a baseline and magnitude.

  2. Burden

    Runcible lists the evidence that phrasing demands under the relevant market's advertising rules.

  3. Test

    Available studies are checked against that burden. One small trial exists; it shows a modest effect.

  4. Repair

    Verdict: repair. Suggested wording: Shown in a clinical study to help reduce X.

  5. Decide

    The responsible regulatory lead accepts the repaired line under their own authority.

  6. Publish & retain

    It ships through the normal approval procedure. The record, and later market response, are kept.

Principles

What we hold ourselves to.

  1. Falsify before admitting. Nothing is trusted because it sounds right.
  2. Bounded, not binary. Verdicts say where they hold and where they stop.
  3. People keep authority. Runcible qualifies; accountable roles decide.
  4. Reasons are reconstructable. Every outcome can be traced and audited later.
  5. Model-agnostic. Use whichever generators you like. We judge the output.
