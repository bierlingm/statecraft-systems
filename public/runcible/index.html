01 / Thesis

Generation is solved. Reliance isn’t.

A model will write anything fluently. Fluency was never the hard part. The hard part is the question a regulator, a court or a citizen asks afterwards: on what grounds did you act?

The vendor doesn’t answer that. You do. Runcible sits at the point of reliance and makes sure there is an answer before anything ships, signs or executes.

02 / The instrument

A compiler for meaning.

SQL made data queryable under a schema. Runcible does the same for claims: it compiles language into typed propositions, then runs them against a governed model of evidence, law and authority.

  1. 01

    Parse

    Language compiles into typed propositions in RDL, Runcible’s semantic language. "Proven" stops being a mood and becomes a burden of evidence.

  2. 02

    Expose

    Every claim smuggles in assumptions. Runcible names them, so nothing is relied on by accident.

  3. 03

    Test

    Five axes, each relative to jurisdiction, evidence and time: truth, possibility, ethics, authority, liability.

  4. 04

    Repair

    Most claims aren’t wrong, they’re overreaching. Runcible returns the strongest version that actually holds.

  5. 05

    Rule & record

    A bounded verdict, an accountable owner, and a Decidability Record that reconstructs the reasoning years later.

05 / Runcible Oversing

The institution,
as one living system.

A verdict is only useful if something accountable acts on it. Oversing connects purpose, people, roles, permissions, procedures, money and history into one live operating picture, then feeds every consequence back into memory.

  1. Purpose sets the program
  2. A role holds the authority
  3. Runcible qualifies the decision
  4. Procedure carries the action
  5. Consequences are observed
  6. Memory updates the institution
  • Live awareness. See how the organisation is actually running, not how the org chart says it does.
  • Capture-resistant. Drift, bloat and politics are visible the moment they start.
  • Continuous adaptation. Reorganise by the week, not by the consultant.
  • Built for agents. A structured world where AI can act inside real permissions.
  • One platform. Coordination without another custom system.
